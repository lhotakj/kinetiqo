Activity Poster Generator & MEGA Stats Infographics

Create wall-art printable posters and customizable summary calendar infographics for your Strava activities.

WYSIWYG Builder

Activity Poster Generator

Design custom posters with elevation profiles, GPS maps, and customizable typography. Powered by Playwright headless chromium for pixel-perfect PNG renders.

  • Aspect ratios: 4:3, 16:9, and 1:1 square ratio
  • Custom resolution export from 800px to 2048px width
  • Background photo caching & custom background uploads
Infographic Visualizer

MEGA Stats & Calendar Heatmaps

Visualize year, half-year, or quarterly progress with Veloviewer-style stats blocks and activity heatmaps. Tailor font sizes, left stats column width, and date formatting (`DATE_FORMAT`).

  • Filter by activity groups (Cycling, Running, Hiking, Multisport)
  • Export high-resolution PNG or vector-ready PDF
  • Browser localStorage state persistence for font & column settings
