# Kinetiqo AI Disclaimer & Usage Guidelines

**Status:** Informational notice  
**Applies to:** AI-powered features, chat, analysis, recommendations, and generated summaries in Kinetiqo

## 1. AI-Generated Content

Kinetiqo may use artificial intelligence (AI) to interpret athletic activity data, identify patterns, summarize training history, and generate insights or recommendations.

AI-generated content is provided for informational and educational purposes only. It is automatically generated and may contain errors, inaccuracies, incomplete interpretations, misleading conclusions, or recommendations that are not appropriate for your individual circumstances.

AI-generated responses should be treated as suggestions to consider, not as verified facts or authoritative assessments of athletic performance.

## 2. Accuracy and Limitations

The quality and reliability of AI-generated results depend on multiple factors, including:

- The accuracy, completeness, and quality of your underlying activity data.
- The amount and consistency of available training history.
- The assumptions, calculations, and limitations of the metrics being analyzed.
- The capabilities, training, reasoning, and limitations of the underlying AI model.
- The instructions, context, and data provided to the AI during a particular interaction.

Different AI models may produce different conclusions when analyzing the same data. Even capable models can misunderstand metrics, overlook relevant context, perform calculations incorrectly, or generate plausible-sounding explanations that are not supported by the evidence.

Kinetiqo does not guarantee the accuracy, completeness, consistency, or suitability of AI-generated outputs.

## 3. Athletic Performance and Training Recommendations

AI-generated insights may help you explore training trends, power profiles, performance changes, training load, fitness estimates, and other aspects of your athletic history.

However, AI-generated recommendations are not personalized coaching services and may not account for all relevant factors, including your training experience, recovery status, nutrition, sleep, medical history, or individual physiological characteristics.

Do not rely exclusively on AI-generated outputs when making significant decisions about training intensity, training volume, recovery, competition preparation, or performance goals.

Use your own judgment and experience, verify important conclusions against the underlying data, and consult a qualified coach or other appropriate professional when necessary.

## 4. No Medical Advice or Diagnosis

Kinetiqo's AI features are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease, injury, or medical condition.

AI-generated content must not be interpreted as medical advice, a medical diagnosis, a clinical assessment, or a validated prediction of injury risk.

Do not use AI-generated insights to make medical decisions or to determine whether you are safe to train or compete. If you experience concerning symptoms, persistent pain, unusual fatigue, or other health concerns, seek advice from an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

## 5. Calculated Metrics vs. AI Interpretations

Kinetiqo may display metrics calculated from recorded activity data using formulas, algorithms, or statistical models, alongside AI-generated interpretations of those metrics.

These are different types of outputs and should not be confused.

A calculated metric may be reproducible under the same inputs and calculation method, but it can still be an estimate, depend on assumptions, or be affected by incomplete or inaccurate source data.

An AI-generated explanation or recommendation introduces additional uncertainty and may misinterpret even correctly calculated metrics.

Where possible, verify numerical values against Kinetiqo's underlying data, metric definitions, and calculation methods rather than relying on an AI-generated description alone.

## 6. User Responsibility

You are responsible for evaluating AI-generated content before acting on it.

You should independently verify important claims, question conclusions that appear inconsistent with your experience, and avoid treating AI-generated outputs as the sole basis for consequential decisions.

Kinetiqo is an analytical tool intended to support your understanding of athletic data. It does not replace your judgment, practical experience, or professional advice where appropriate.

## 7. No Guarantee of Results

Kinetiqo's AI features are provided on an informational basis, without a guarantee that any particular insight, recommendation, prediction, or interpretation will be correct or useful.

The usefulness of the output may vary depending on the available data, the task being performed, and the AI model used. AI-generated content may change when the model, its configuration, the available context, or the underlying data changes.

## 8. Feedback and Continuous Improvement

If you identify an inaccurate, misleading, or unsupported AI-generated conclusion, consider verifying it against the original activity data and reporting the issue through the project's available feedback channels.

Feedback helps identify weaknesses and opportunities to improve the reliability and usefulness of Kinetiqo's AI features.
